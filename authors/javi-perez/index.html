Author Profile

About Javi Pérez

Javi Pérez is the founder and sole editor of CreditCostGuide. He is an independent publisher based in Almería, Spain who builds long-form educational resources for U.S. consumers on credit, loans, mortgages, banking fees, refinancing, and debt payoff.

His background is technical — operating digital products and editorial sites — not regulated financial services. Javi Pérez is not a licensed financial advisor, CPA, CFP, loan officer, tax professional, or attorney. This content is educational only and does not replace advice from a qualified professional.

Find Javi on LinkedIn: https://www.linkedin.com/in/javi-perez-guides. For corrections, source updates, or partnership inquiries, email javiperezguides@gmail.com.

Editorial Approach

How Javi works

  • Primary-source first. Every page links to at least three regulator or government sources (CFPB, Federal Reserve, FDIC, BLS, FTC, SEC, studentaid.gov).
  • AI-assisted drafting, human review. Drafts are produced with large-language-model help, then revised, fact-checked, and signed off by Javi before publishing.
  • Quarterly review. Every indexable guide is revisited at least every 90 days; rate-sensitive numbers are checked monthly against the original federal data series.
  • No personalized advice. CreditCostGuide does not collect reader data, does not match readers with lenders, and is not a registered investment adviser.
